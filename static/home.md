I'm Umarbek, cool-things enthusiast, creating whatever my heart desires in the world of digital.

As you might have noticed (ugh ugh... by the style of this whole website), I love [Blender](https://www.blender.org). While the level of artistic skill of mine is work in progress, the passion and dedication is definitely there.

Recently, together with my team, I've been working on [matetra](https://matetra.org), the ultimate math-based card game. Essentially, the goal of the game is to maximise numbers in your set while minimising ones of your opponents by combining constant, function, and theorem cards to create expressions for attacking / defending. Feel free to check it out if you love maths as much as I do!

Inspired from the pantheon show, me and [Esia](https://esia.dev) worked on [MIST](https://github.com/flushloll/MIST)—general purpose quadraped robot in a form of a rice-cooker. My part of the project was the wiring/electrical engineering and sourcing components as well as writing custom ROS (for which I used 100% Go, just to demonstrate my love towards this beautiful language). While the blueprints are finished, in the end we didn't end up making the project in real life due to time constraints, but all the assembly and firmware flashing instructions and source files for 3d printing are open source! If you're planning to build it, please contact one of us beforehand, we'd love to assist and ensure it goes smoothly.
